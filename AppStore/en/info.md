## Description
<!-- Max 4000 characters -->
Make your Mac truly yours. Colir lets you customize any app icon with colors, photos, gradients, and emojis — all with a simple drag-and-drop workflow.

How It Works:
Drop any app onto Colir, apply your customizations, and export a brand-new icon. Real-time preview shows every change instantly. Full undo/redo support means you can experiment freely.

Customize Your Way:
• Apply any color with an interactive color wheel
• Use photos from your library as icon backgrounds
• Add emoji overlays with skin tone variants
• Combine multiple styles for unique results

Flexible Icon Sources:
• Extract icons from any macOS app
• Browse your Pictures folder
• Access photos directly from the Photos app

Privacy First:
• All processing happens locally on your Mac
• No data collected or sent anywhere
• Works completely offline
• Photos accessed read-only

Transform your dock, organize your apps visually, or just have fun making your Mac look exactly the way you want.
